# Warzone Sens Calibrator

A browser tool that finds your Call of Duty: Warzone mouse sensitivity from how your flicks land.

Flick to each target and click it. The page measures where your first fast movement stops, past the target or short of it, and adjusts your sensitivity after each round until two rounds in a row land within 6% of the target.

- Uses Warzone's sensitivity scale (0.0066° per count at sens 1), so the result goes straight into the game at the same DPI.
- Shows a replay after each flick: where the target was, your path, and where the flick stopped.
- Includes a 10 cm ruler check to confirm the browser reads your DPI correctly.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on desktop, fullscreen, with Windows pointer speed at 6/11 and Enhance pointer precision off.
