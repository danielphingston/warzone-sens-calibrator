How it works

Three drills, one number. Each drill tests a spread of sens values and scores how well you aim with each. A curve through those scores finds your peak, kept inside a range your arm and mousepad can handle unless you turn the limits off. Open Settings to change the scoring method; each one explains what it measures and why.

Flicks

Targets 20–90° away. Your first click ends each target, hit or miss, so speed and accuracy are both measured. The replay marks where your first fast movement stopped (X) and where the target was (dashed ring).

Tracking

Keep the crosshair on a target that strafes like a player 10–20 m away (8–28°/s by default, direction changes every 0.6–1.5 s). The first 220 ms after each direction change isn't scored, since nobody can react faster than that. Always scored by time on target.

Micro-adjust

Small targets 3–12° away, like correcting onto a head. Scored with the selected method (Fitts throughput when Reach matching is selected).

Practice

Turn on Practice, then pick Flicks, Tracking or Micro-adjust under Test to drill it at exactly your Center sens, for as long as you like. Live hit rate, time and where your flicks stop show in the corner. Nothing is scored or saved, and the ergonomic limits don't apply.

Overall sens

Tracking 45%, flicks 35%, micro-adjust 20%, blended on a log scale and kept inside your ergonomic range. Only drills you've run count.
