Export for AI analysis

Builds one block of text with an analysis prompt, your settings, every block and every target with its raw readings, and field definitions. Paste it into Claude or another AI to get a second opinion on your sens.

Flicks

Targets 20–90° away. Your first click ends each target, hit or miss, so speed and accuracy are both measured. The replay marks where your first fast movement stopped (X) and where the target was (dashed ring).

Tracking

Keep the crosshair on a target that strafes and changes direction like a player. Always scored by time on target, whatever method you pick.

Micro-adjust

Small targets 3–12° away, like correcting onto a head. Scored with the selected method (Fitts throughput when Reach matching is selected).

Overall sens

Tracking 45%, flicks 35%, micro-adjust 20%, blended on a log scale and kept inside your ergonomic range. Only drills you've run count.
