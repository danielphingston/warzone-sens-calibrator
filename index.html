Warzone Sens Calibrator

Three drills, one number. Flick reach checks whether your snaps land past or short of the target. Tracking and micro-adjust test five sens values each and find the one you aim best with. The full test runs all three and blends them into your overall sens.

Flick reach

Snap to each target in one motion. The X in the replay marks where your first fast movement stopped. Overshooting means the sens is too fast for your hand, stopping short means too slow. Sens adjusts each round until two rounds land within 6%.

Tracking

Keep the crosshair on a target that strafes and changes direction like a player. Five sens values, two blocks each, in shuffled order. The sens with the most time on target wins, smoothed with a curve fit so one lucky block can't decide it.

Micro-adjust

Small, close targets, like correcting onto a head at mid range. Same five-value test, scored by targets hit per minute with a penalty for misses. Too high a sens shows up here first.

Overall sens

Tracking counts 45%, flick reach 35% and micro-adjust 20%, because most Warzone fights are won by staying on target. Only drills you've run are included. Run the full test on another day to confirm.
