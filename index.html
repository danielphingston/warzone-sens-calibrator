Flick, don't creep

Move to each target in one quick motion, the way you'd snap to an enemy. Slow, careful aiming hides overshoot and undershoot.

Reading the replay

After each hit, the dashed ring shows where the target was and the X shows where your first fast movement stopped. The % is how far along the line to the target that X is. Corrections and the click itself don't count, and missing high or low is shown separately because it doesn't change your sens.

Reach, not score

Overshooting means your sens is too fast for your hand; stopping short means it's too slow. The page stops when two rounds in a row land within 6% of the target.

Run it twice

Muscle memory pulls toward your old sens. Run it again on another day, and if you're deliberately moving slower, treat the result as the top of your range.
